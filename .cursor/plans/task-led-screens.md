# Task-led screens (ADR-039)

**Status:** Approved by the owner on 2026-11-15 after comparing the mock-ups at `/design-lab/next-ideas` (fictional data). T1, T2, T3 and the reference property page (S1, S2) are built. **The whole site moves to the card style before Stage B viewings (owner decision 2026-11-15); the order is in "Whole-site rollout" below, which replaces the T4 to T6 order in the table.** Fictional data only.

## Why

The 2026-11-15 review found the screens consistent but built around records, not the agent's job. The owner approved three ideas: a journey bar with one Next step on records (with a light header), lists you work from, and a dashboard Home that is the person's day.

## Whole-site rollout (owner decision 2026-11-15)

After seeing the mock-up beside the real pages, the owner asked for the whole site in the mock-up's style: white cards, a calm header, the journey, one next step, plain detail rows and a timeline. Record pages therefore use cards, reversing ADR-030's flat sections (recorded in ADR-039). Viewings wait until this is done so they are built once.

Per record page, the same questions are answered from data already loaded: where it is (journey, where the record has one), the one next step, what needs attention, what happened (timeline), key details, people. Header buttons become `variant="outline"`.

**Limits of the first timeline (S2):** it is put together from the records the property page already loads (when the property was added, instructions starting and ending, each listing's latest state, notes, branch moves, owners starting and ending). It is not a log of every action, so it cannot say "added 14 photos", and most entries have no name against them. A fuller timeline with names needs a merged read and its own review (was T6); the instruction record (S3) already holds named history and is the place to start.

## Notes and open points

- **Order against viewings (Stage B), owner decision 2026-11-15:** T2 (Your day), then T3 (working list on Landlord leads), then Stage B viewings, then T5 (journey bar) once viewings exist so its Viewings stage is real. T4 and T6 are placed when those are done.
- **Do not invent rules.** The journey and Next step only reflect existing records and gates. "Needs attention" repeats the existing compliance attention items; it never decides compliance.
- **Phone:** the journey bar scrolls sideways inside its own box and opens with the current stage in the middle (built in S1).
- **Property header buttons:** Edit property, Manage owners and Archive are still three buttons; the mock-up had Edit and a More menu. Folding the rarely used ones into More is open.
- **Photos on property and listing rows** were raised in the review and not mocked up; they wait for the thumbnail work already recorded in the marketing plan.
- **Dashboard tabs:** Your day replaces the Home tab only. The long Landlords & instructions tab is reviewed after T3, when the working list may make most of it unnecessary.
- **Clean-up when the Design Lab goes:** remove the `record` colour tokens and their contrast pairs.
