# ADR-039: Task-led screens: a light record header, the journey bar, working lists and Your day

- **Status:** Accepted (owner decision, 2026-11-15), subject to the staged rollout in `.cursor/plans/task-led-screens.md`. Amends the record header in ADR-038; the rest of ADR-038 (Crisp Slate fields and palette, the sidebar, create in a modal) stands.
- **Context:** A product and design review on 2026-11-15 found the screens tidy and consistent but built around records rather than the letting agent's job: no screen showed where a property is in its journey or what to do next, the solid blue record box drew the eye to static facts, lists needed dropdowns and an Apply button, and the dashboard Home was mostly counts. Three options were drawn in the Design Lab with fictional data (`/design-lab/next-ideas`, development only) and the owner approved all three, choosing the light header over the blue box after comparing both.
- **Decision:**
  - **Record header:** every record page keeps the shared `RecordHeader`, now a **white box with a border** (`bg-card`) instead of the solid blue box. Colour on a record is kept for status and for what needs doing. The `record` colour tokens stay only for the Design Lab comparison and are removed with it.
  - **Journey bar:** records that belong to a property's journey show a strip of stages (Lead, Appraisal, Instruction, Listing, Enquiries, Viewings, Let) with the current one marked, and **one Next step panel** beside anything that needs attention. Stages that Zayto does not have yet are shown as still to come, never as done. The journey is derived from existing records; it adds no rules of its own and does not replace any gate.
  - **Working lists:** list pages use **views with live counts** in place of filter dropdowns and Apply, filter as the person types, and open a **preview beside the list** for the selected row with its main actions.
  - **Your day:** the dashboard Home leads with one ordered list of what needs the signed-in person (Now, Today, This week), each line with a single action; the figures become one slim strip.
  - **One timeline per record** and **hiding empty details behind one link** are approved in principle and follow the stages above.
- **Consequences:** Every new read (journey, views with counts, the day list, the timeline) must use the existing workspace, branch and permission checks and show a person nothing they could not already open. The work is staged so each part is checked in light, dark and phone before the next, and it is scheduled alongside Stage B (viewings) rather than replacing it. `.cursor/rules/ui-design.mdc` is updated with each stage as it lands.
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
