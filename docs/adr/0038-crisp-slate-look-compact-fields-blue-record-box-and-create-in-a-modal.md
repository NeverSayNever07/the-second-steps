# ADR-038: Crisp Slate look: compact fields, a blue record box, and create in a modal

- **Status:** Accepted (owner decision, 2026-11-08), subject to the rollout in `.cursor/plans/look-and-feel-rollout.md`. Partly supersedes ADR-017 (visual direction) and amends ADR-030 and the modal guidance in `.cursor/rules/ui-design.mdc`.
- **Context:** After using the built screens, the owner found the large, heavily rounded fields out of place, fields filled with the same grey as the page, page headers spread out, and record pages grey with nothing to draw the eye. Two rounds of options were compared in the Design Lab (`/design-lab/look-and-feel` and `/design-lab/look-and-feel-2`, development only), drawn with the real shadcn components.
- **Decision:**
  - **Fields (Crisp Slate):** inputs, selects, textareas and buttons are compact (about 32px tall) with a small corner radius (about 6px, "slightly square but still a slight curve") and a **white fill** with a visible border, never the page grey. Their shape comes from the shared `components/ui` primitives, not per-screen classes.
  - **Palette:** Crisp Slate replaces the Deep Navy palette from ADR-017: a cool grey canvas with white surfaces, one confident blue as the primary colour, and a **dark navy sidebar**. The navigation stays a **sidebar** (a top bar was tried and rejected). Contacts is a group containing All contacts, Landlords and Applicants.
  - **Record header:** every record page has a **solid blue box** at the top holding the avatar or icon, the name (a property's address), the reference, status badges and the primary actions (as secondary buttons). The record's **key facts sit inside the box** as a second row of icon, label and value, separated from the title by a faint line. This replaces the separate five-box facts strip. The profile-sidebar layout from ADR-030 is kept for the working area and details.
  - **List pages:** a compact header (title and count on the left, the create button on the right) followed by one toolbar row for search and filters, then the table. The table treatment is unchanged.
  - **Create forms are modals.** Every "create" form (a landlord lead, a contact, a property, an applicant, and so on) opens as a short modal from the list or record it belongs to. On save, the new record opens with a confirmation. A modal holds the essential fields only; the rest are added on the record afterwards.
- **Exceptions to the modal rule:** the existing guidance stands where a task is long, multi-stage or needs substantial context (for example the instruction terms and agreement). Those stay full pages or step-by-step flows unless the owner decides otherwise case by case. A create flow that needs a duplicate-review step (landlord lead, contact, applicant) does it as a second step inside the modal.
- **Consequences:** This changes the live look of every screen, so it is rolled out in stages, starting with design tokens and the shared components, then list and record pages, then converting create forms. The colour changes must keep the contrast checks in `styles/theme-contrast.test.ts` passing in light and dark, and a phone shows each create modal as a bottom sheet (ADR-018). Dashboards are out of scope for now and keep their current look until the owner decides.
- **Date:** 2026-11-08
