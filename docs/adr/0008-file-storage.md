# ADR-008: File storage

- **Status:** Accepted for the fictional-data proof of concept (owner decision, 2026-10-20); implementation and real-data release remain gated
- **Decision:** Use Supabase Storage in the existing London project for private documents, beginning with fictional externally signed landlord-agreement PDFs. Buckets are private; documents never use `/public`, a public bucket, a permanent public URL or a browser-facing Supabase link. Authorised downloads pass through the Zayto server after a fresh access check (owner decision, 2026-10-20).
- **Application boundary:** The browser does not receive a Supabase client or secret and does not decide storage access. Existing workspace, branch, role and record permissions are checked by the Zayto server before upload, listing, download, replacement or deletion. A storage object path, possession of a record ID or uploader identity is never sufficient authority. The Supabase server secret can bypass Storage RLS, so it remains server-only and every operation must pass application authorisation; Storage policies provide defence in depth rather than replacing that check.
- **Bucket and object layout:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** use one private documents bucket for every workspace rather than provisioning a bucket per agency. Use an opaque path shaped from internal workspace, document and immutable version identifiers (for example `<workspace-id>/<document-id>/<version-id>.pdf`); never include an agency, person, property or original filename. The path is organisation and reconciliation, not an access-control boundary. The database record is authoritative for workspace/record ownership and the server checks it before addressing the object.
- **Document boundary:** Store an opaque object key and immutable file version in Storage; keep workspace ownership, record links, classification, original display name, media type, size, digest, review state, creator and audit history in Zayto's database. Do not place personal data in an object path. **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** a completed agreement PDF and its exact terms snapshot are immutable. A correction, changed party or changed material term creates a linked replacement or variation and preserves the original and supersession history; no upload or edit overwrites the completed artefact.
- **Upload controls:** The first agreement upload accepts PDF only, with a maximum size of 10 MB (owner decision, 2026-10-20). Password-protected or encrypted PDFs are rejected; Zayto does not request or store a PDF password. **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** supporting compliance evidence accepts PDF, JPEG and PNG only, also up to 10 MB per file; HEIC and other formats wait. Enforce the applicable type and size limits at the private bucket and independently on the server; validate actual content/signature and PDF encryption state rather than trusting the filename, extension or browser-supplied media type. **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** malware-scanning infrastructure is deferred for the fictional MVP. Until an upload scanning/quarantine design is implemented and reviewed, accept only controlled, non-sensitive test files supplied for the test journey; arbitrary, untrusted or real uploads remain prohibited. Do not inline-preview or otherwise process uploaded content in this interim path.
- **Delivery controls:** The browser requests the document from an authenticated Zayto route. The server re-verifies workspace, branch, role, record and document permission, fetches the private object server-to-server, and streams it as a download with safe headers. Audit successful and refused sensitive document access without logging content. Do not create or expose a Supabase signed URL to the browser. Keep responses non-cacheable and do not inline-preview agreement PDFs in the first version.
- **Agreement viewing permission:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** every active staff role that can read an instruction within its verified workspace and branch scope may view/download that instruction's signed agreement PDF, including Negotiator, Property Manager, Accounts and Read Only. This does not give upload, completion, replacement or deletion permission and does not grant access to landlords or other external participants. A future portal needs explicit resource/document grants.
- **Compliance-document viewing permission:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** every active staff role authorised to read a property within verified workspace and branch scope may view/download its supporting compliance documents, including Accounts and Read Only. This does not grant compliance-edit or document-change permission and grants no external-participant access.
- **Compliance evidence versions:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** one compliance item may link multiple immutable evidence files and events, such as an inspection report and later remedial-work confirmation. The compliance record identifies the evidence currently relied upon while preserving older, superseded and supporting files as history. Replacing the current evidence never overwrites or silently deletes an earlier object.
- **Agreement change permission:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** Account Owner, Agency Admin, Branch Manager, Negotiator and Property Manager may upload and mark an agreement complete within the instruction's verified workspace and branch scope, matching `instruction.manage`. Accounts and Read Only cannot upload, complete, replace or otherwise change agreement evidence. Replacement/variation and deletion remain separately controlled decisions.
- **Removal and supersession:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** a user with `instruction.manage` may remove an incorrect draft upload before the agreement is completed, with an audit event and safe object cleanup. A completed agreement cannot be deleted through the normal interface; correction creates a linked replacement or variation and marks the earlier agreement superseded without removing its evidence. Permanent erasure is limited to a later approved retention/erasure process or controlled fictional-workspace cleanup.
- **External execution evidence:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** for the first outside-Zayto signing workflow, the system automatically records uploader, upload time, verified type/size, storage version and document digest. Staff confirm that the document contains every required signature and enter one overall signed/completed date shown by the document; they do not duplicate a signing method and timestamp for each signer. If the document has no clear signed/completed date, Zayto refuses completion until a dated copy is supplied; staff cannot invent or substitute a date. A future Zayto-owned signing journey must capture each signer's authenticated event, intent, timestamp and delivery evidence automatically.
- **Fictional retention:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** completed fictional agreement files and their records remain until the fictional workspace is explicitly reset; the MVP has no automatic document-deletion schedule. Reset cleanup must remove both metadata and the corresponding private objects and record/verify the outcome. A legally and operationally reviewed retention, erasure and legal-hold policy is mandatory before real documents.
- **Fictional recovery:** **Owner decision (2026-10-20):** the fictional MVP has no separate file backup/restore system. Controlled test PDFs are disposable and may be re-uploaded after loss. The interface and internal readiness reporting must not imply that fictional uploads are durably backed up. Before real documents, approve a suitable paid service level and backup scope, document recovery objectives and reconciliation, and successfully test restore/recovery.
- **Still unresolved before real or arbitrary uploads:** upload scanning/quarantine; retention, erasure and legal-hold; and backup/restore/reconciliation. **Still unresolved before the fictional implementation:** final bucket name/configuration; workspace-reset cleanup; storage metadata/RLS design; safe download headers/streaming limits; and failure/orphan cleanup.
- **Real-data gate:** Before any real landlord, applicant, tenant, property or agreement document is accepted, approve the unresolved controls, provider/DPA and retention position, move to a suitable paid/backed-up service level, run `review-security`, and pass cross-workspace, cross-branch, role, object-substitution, upload-validation and download-expiry tests.
