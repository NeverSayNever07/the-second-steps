# ADR-034: Listings advance to Ready to advertise inside Zayto only

- **Status:** Accepted for the fictional-data proof of concept (owner decision, 2026-10-21); legal validation of the gates remains a real-data requirement
- **Context:** A Ready to market instruction had no next step. The owner asked for the marketing phase to be planned with photos, ending at an internal "Ready to advertise" state, with no publishing yet. Earlier marketing notes used different state names, put the EPC gate one step earlier than the compliance plan, and proposed a second copy of EPC evidence on the listing. Design: `.cursor/plans/marketing-m0-design.md`.
- **Decision:**
  - **A listing is a separate record** linked to one instruction and one property, created by an explicit **Prepare listing** action on a current Ready to market instruction (never automatically). There is one current listing per instruction; history is kept.
  - **States:** Draft, Content ready, Ready to advertise, Withdrawn. **Ready to advertise is an internal state.** It does not mean the property is published, and no screen, label or message may say or imply that a listing is live, published or accepted anywhere. Advertised and portal states need a real, verified publication action and a later decision.
  - **Content is versioned and immutable.** Each explicit save is a new version; the highest version number is current. Rent changes, readiness changes and withdrawals are append-only events with actor, time and reason. Any edit after review returns the listing to Draft.
  - **Advertised rent is read-only** and copied from the instruction's agreed terms; a change in the source is shown as drift for the user to accept. Landlord-private data, fees, the agreement, the ownership check, parties and internal notes are never copied (an explicit allowlist enforces this).
  - **Instruction coupling is in the database:** ending the instruction withdraws the current listing and returning it to Onboarding takes Content ready and Ready to advertise back to Draft, in the same transaction, by a trigger on the instruction, so no caller can forget it.
  - **EPC:** Content ready records the property's EPC position for the reviewer; the hard block is at Ready to advertise, as in the compliance plan. The listing snapshots the property's EPC evidence position and shows drift; it never owns a second copy of compliance records. Ready to advertise needs reviewed unexpired evidence, a commissioned/awaiting state, or a reasoned Does not apply, and is blocked by Not assessed, Needs specialist review, Action required, expired evidence, or a relied-on rating of F or G (unless Does not apply with a recorded reason). No role overrides it. Other compliance gaps are warnings only.
  - **Minimum media** for Ready to advertise: 5 photos and 1 floorplan, the first photo being the main image, held as constants.
  - **Permissions:** `listing.read` for all staff within branch scope; `listing.prepare` and `listing.review` for Account Owner, Agency Admin, Branch Manager and Negotiator.
- **Consequences:** The workflow can be built and shown to agencies without external dependencies. Public photo delivery, portals, a website feed and AI copy are separate later decisions. The F/G representation (Does not apply with a reason) is an interim rule pending an exemption marker from the legal review. ADR-008 is amended for staff-only photo preview (see that record).
- **Date:** 2026-10-21
